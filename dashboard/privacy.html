Privacy

Last updated 24 November 2026

This page says what the Embed Dashboard and its Discord bot store, why, and how to have it removed.

Who runs it

The 9 Nation team runs the dashboard (this website) and the bot. Ask for help or removal through the owner of the server you use it in, or through the bot owner on Discord.

What is stored

  • Your Discord user ID, only to check that you may use a server, to enforce cooldown and once-per-person rules on buttons you press, and to record the time you last logged out.
  • The messages you design: their text, images' web addresses, buttons, dropdowns, forms, replies, names, schedules, saved templates, webhook name and picture presets, the server and channel IDs they are for, and the last 20 saved versions of each message.
  • Approved server list: server IDs and names.

What is not stored

  • No passwords, Discord tokens, email addresses or message content from your servers' members.
  • Answers to pop-up forms are sent straight to the Discord channel the server chose. The bot does not keep a copy.
  • Your IP address is used only briefly in memory to limit abuse and is not saved.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no cookies. The dashboard keeps a login pass in your browser's local storage. Pressing Log out removes it and cancels it on the server.

Where it is kept

On a free bot host. A copy of the database is also saved every night in a private repository for recovery, and the newest 14 copies are kept.

Sharing

Data is never sold or shared with anyone. It is not used for anything except running this tool.

Deleting your data

Delete any message in the dashboard to remove it, its versions, its schedules and its button records. To remove everything for a server or for yourself, ask the owner and it will be deleted. Backup copies disappear within 14 days.
