About

Last updated 24 November 2026

The Embed Dashboard is a private tool for designing, posting, editing and scheduling rich Discord messages (embeds, buttons, dropdowns, pop-up forms and role buttons). It is run by the 9 Nation team for Discord servers it has approved.

Who can use it

Only the owner, and Administrators of servers that the owner has approved. The bot leaves any server that is not approved. If you want your server added, ask the owner.

What it is not

It is an embed bot only. It has no tickets, moderation, levels, polls or giveaways. It is not made by, affiliated with or endorsed by Discord Inc.

How logging in works

You log in with Discord. The tool asks only for the identify permission, which tells it your Discord user ID. It never asks for your password, and it cannot read your messages, servers list or email.
