Quick Tour & Guides

1. Pick a Server & Create
Choose your server at the top of My messages, then click New (Layout, Classic embed, or Starter gallery).
2. Build & Format
Click parts in the preview or tree to edit. Use the 2-row toolbar for Bold, Links, Headings, and {x} Var to insert live Discord variables like {{usermention}} or {{servername}}.
3. Buttons, Replies, Forms & Pages
Buttons can open links, grant/toggle roles, show private replies, open pop-up forms, or flip Pages. Test everything in Test mode before posting!
4. Send, Schedule & Sequences
Use Send to post or update, Schedule for timed/recurring posts, and Server tools for message sequences, templates, and backups.
