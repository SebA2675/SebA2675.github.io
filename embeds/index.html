How to Get Started

Three quick steps to start building messages for your Discord server.

1. Add the App

Click Add App on the bot's Discord profile to invite it to your server.

2. Get Approved

Currently invite-only. Join our support server and ask to approve your server.

3. Log In & Build

Open the Dashboard and sign in with Discord (Admins & Editors).
