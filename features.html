Everything Included

Every server gets its own independent workspace for messages, automation, and tools.

Components V2 & Classic Embeds

Build modern accented Boxes, Sections, Image Galleries (up to 10), Dividers, Button Rows, and Dropdowns — or up to 10 Classic Embeds per message.

Formatting & Custom Emojis

1-click Markdown toolbar, live character counter, problem checker, and a Custom Emoji Picker pulling animated & static emojis from your servers.

Permanent & Custom Variables

Use {{usermention}}, {{servername}}, {{membercount}}, and up to 20 custom variables with a live Variable Playground.

Interactive Forms & CSV Export

Attach 5-question pop-up Discord modals to buttons, log answers to a staff channel with Accept/Deny buttons & auto-roles, and export CSV.

Self-Role Wizard & Multi-Page

Generate button or dropdown role menus in seconds (with admin-role safety blocks), plus paginated messages and role/cooldown access rules.

Welcome, Keywords & Feeds

Set up Welcome/Goodbye, Keyword auto-replies, RSS/YouTube/Reddit feeds, Sticky messages, /faq commands, and recurring Schedules.

Webhooks, Brand Kit & Sequences

Post under custom webhook personas, auto-apply your server's default colour & footer, and send up to 10 messages in sequence.

Imports, Backups & Teamwork

Import from Discord message links or Discohook JSON, copy layouts across servers, grant Editor access, and restore from 30-day Trash.
